# Edestus — dávka 07

Vestavěný Imagegen, nová původní scéna bez cizího obrazového vstupu.

Realistic Edestus heinrichi in a Carboniferous blue-green sea, 3:2. Full animal in a three-quarter front-side view; visibly toothless lateral jaw margins; exactly one longitudinal median dental blade in each jaw. Upper crowns point down, lower up; flattened triangular serrated crowns with connected bases; gentle inward curvature, no giant external scissors and no spiral. Conservative body, complete fins and tail, no labels, borders or artificial background padding. Color and soft anatomy conjectural. Based on Tapanila et al. 2020.
